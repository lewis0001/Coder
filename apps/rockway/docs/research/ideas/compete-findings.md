# Rockway — Competitive findings & strategic wedge

> Done directly (the competitive agents hit a session rate-limit). Sources are
> live web searches, June 2026. The headline changes our positioning.

## The competitive landscape (verified)
- **Gib Local (giblocal.com)** — the closest competitor. Free app advertising
  *"border wait times, live bus tracking, traffic alerts, events, flights,
  weather, parking and a directory of local services."* That is almost exactly
  Rockway's **territory-data layer**. ⇒ Those feeds are **table-stakes, not a moat**.
- **GoGoGibraltar** — border times + flights + weather dashboard.
- **Queue Wisely / Queue Ninja** — crowd/algorithm border-time apps.
- **frontierqueue.gi / gibraltarborder.gi** — official live cameras + hotline.
- **GibYellow** — business directory / yellow-pages (listings, not booking).
- Food delivery (Hungry Monkey, NomNoms) — delivery only, not services/booking.
- Classifieds/property/jobs — Facebook groups + portals (Property Gibraltar,
  recruitgibraltar) — fragmented, no booking, no unified app.

## What this means — Rockway's real wedge
We should **match** the territory feeds (we largely do: frontier/weather/runway/
news/today) but stop treating them as the differentiator. The defensible wedge,
which the incumbents do NOT have, is:
1. **Two-sided booking marketplace** (Discover + For Business) — nobody local does
   real service booking; GibYellow is a static directory. THIS is the moat.
2. **Community / UGC layer** — car-pool, classifieds, reviews, lost & found.
3. **Design quality** — the "limestone & key" / living-Rock identity vs utilitarian rivals.
4. **"Ask Rockway" concierge** — one box that *acts* on our own data.

Implication for build order: lead with **booking depth + community + concierge**,
not "another border-times screen." Keep the live feeds excellent but framed as
supporting cast.

## Idea validation verdicts
- **Frontier car-pool — VIABLE, white space (VERIFIED gap).** ~36,000 people /
  ~10,000 vehicles cross daily; no dedicated car-pool app exists — coordination is
  ad-hoc Facebook + park-and-walk. Caveat: *walking* is the dominant mode for
  central-La-Línea workers, so car-pool serves the subset who drive from down the
  coast (Estepona/Manilva/San Roque) — still thousands. Honest MVP = a **lift board**
  (offers + requests + chat), **no money movement** (cost-sharing only, off-platform)
  to stay clear of taxi/PSP licensing. Beat Facebook with: recurring commute slots,
  direction/time filters, reputation, and a one-tap "going my way?" match.
- **Ask Rockway concierge — VIABLE now (no LLM).** Retrieval over our registry +
  search providers + live feeds; differentiator is composing *our* data
  ("groomer free Saturday + how's the frontier?"). Frame as smart search, add a
  Haiku routing layer only once query logs justify it.
- **Booking improvements (vs Booksy/Fresha/OpenTable)** to punch above weight:
  real per-business availability (done), reminders, **"book again"**, waitlist for
  full slots, and a lightweight business analytics line. Deposits later (needs PSP).
- **Live feeds** — keep, but they're parity features now, not the pitch.

## New ideas the scan surfaced
1. **"Park & Walk" helper** — for the huge cohort who drive to La Línea and walk
   across: where to park Spanish-side + live frontier pedestrian status + walk time.
2. **Directory claim flow** (vs GibYellow): "is this your business? claim & take
   bookings" — converts a static-directory habit into our two-sided marketplace.
3. **Commuter mode** — a personalised home for the cross-border worker: frontier
   both ways + car-pool + bus + payday/Spanish-holiday flags.
4. **Unified "what's affecting me"** vs scattered rival alerts — one honest feed.
5. **Business analytics for owners** — views, booking conversion, repeat-rate;
   the hook that retains the supply side that GibYellow/Facebook can't.

## Posture
Don't out-feature Gib Local on border times — **out-class** on booking, community,
concierge and design. Border data is the doorway; the marketplace is the business.
