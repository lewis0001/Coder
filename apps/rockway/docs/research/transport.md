# Transport & Mobility in Gibraltar

Research for the Rockway "everything app." Compiled 2026-05-30. Facts are cited; figures that change frequently (fares, schedules) are flagged as needing live verification.

## Taxis

- **Single operator, no ride-hailing.** Effectively all licensed taxis belong to the **Gibraltar Taxi Association (GTA)**; drivers are owner-operators. There are **no minicabs and no app-based ride-hailing** — Uber, Bolt and Cabify do **not** operate in Gibraltar (confirmed as of 2025). Licensed taxis are the only taxi-style option in the territory. [TripAdvisor], [Expat Focus]
- **Regulation.** Taxis are regulated under the Transport Act 1998 and overseen by the **Gibraltar Transport Commission**; fares are set by government regulation (Taxi Fares Regulations 2022, with updated **New Taxis Regulations 270/2025**). [gibraltarlaws.gov.gi], [gibraltar.gov.gi]
- **How to hail/book.** The normal method is a **taxi rank** rather than an app. Main ranks are at **Grand Casemates Square** and **Cathedral Square** (Main Street, behind the Anglican cathedral), plus the airport, frontier and cruise terminal. Taxis can also be phoned/pre-booked via the GTA. [Expat Focus]
- **Fares / zones.** Standard journeys are metered, with tariffs fixed by government. Indicative (verify against current regulated tariff): base fare ~£3–4 and ~£2–3 per km (2025 estimate). For airport/pre-booked runs it is common to agree the price upfront. *(Exact regulated tariff and zone bands should be pulled from the live Transport Commission schedule.)* [Expat Focus], [Numbeo]
- **Rock tours by taxi.** A major part of GTA business is **Upper Rock Nature Reserve tours** (drivers must still accept ordinary metered fares too). Indicative pricing:
  - Shared/group minibus tour (up to ~8 people): **~£22–25 per person**, often including the Nature Reserve ticket.
  - Private standard tour: **~£360 per vehicle** (up to 6 people), Nature Reserve entry included.
  - VIP unlimited tour: **~£200/hour**, 2-hour minimum.
  Tour prices are agreed with the driver (not metered); tours visit Europa Point, Ape's Den, St Michael's Cave, the Great Siege Tunnels, etc. [TripAdvisor], [GTA Tours], [Buy Tickets Gibraltar]

## Buses

- **Operators.** **Gibraltar Bus Company** (government-owned) runs most routes; **Calypso Transport** runs the frontier shuttle. Eight routes total. [Wikipedia: Transport in Gibraltar]
- **Routes (numbers + destinations):**
  - **Route 1** — Moorish Castle Estate / Upper Town area.
  - **Route 2** — Referendum House ↔ Willis's Road.
  - **Route 3** — Air Terminal ↔ Europa Point.
  - **Route 4** — Rosia ↔ Both Worlds.
  - **Route 5** *(Calypso, red double-deckers)* — **Frontier (Spain border) / Airport ↔ city centre / Market Place**; the **only route serving the frontier and airport**. Runs every ~15 min Mon–Sat, ~20 min Sun/bank holidays.
  - **Routes 7, 8, 9** — additional local routes (e.g. 9: Air Terminal ↔ Market Place). [Wikipedia], [Calypso/Angloinfo]
  *(Route list/termini shift periodically — pull live data from gibraltarbuscompany.gi and track.bus.gi.)*
- **Fares (current, verify):** Adult single **£1.80**, all-day pass **£2.50**; pensioner **£1.00 / £1.50**; child **£1.50 / £2.00**. **Qualifying residents, commuters and military personnel travel free.** A **Hopper** ticket gives unlimited travel for the day (also sold to tourists). [Wikipedia]
- **How you pay.** On-board ticket purchase; day/Hopper passes; Hopper tickets purchasable online. A live **bus tracker** exists at **track.bus.gi**. [gibraltarbuscompany.gi]

## Cable Car

- **Operator.** Run as part of the **Gibraltar Nature Reserve** (naturereserve.gi). Ride is ~412 m to the Top Station in ~6 minutes.
- **CURRENT STATUS — CLOSED.** The cable car is **temporarily closed for a full refurbishment and is expected to reopen in 2027** (so **not operational in 2026**). In the meantime visitors reach the Upper Rock by **taxi tour or on foot**. *(This is the single most important time-sensitive fact in this document.)* [naturereserve.gi]
- **Hours (when operating):** Mon–Sun ~09:30, last car up ~19:15 / down ~19:45 (seasonal variation; the reserve has also published 09:30–18:15 with last entry 17:45). [naturereserve.gi]
- **Prices (pre-closure / on reopening, verify):** Adult round trip incl. Nature Reserve ~**£49.00**; adult one-way incl. reserve ~**£46.50**; child (4–11) round trip ~**£31.00**. [naturereserve.gi]
- **At the top / nearby:** Top Station with café, **Mons Calpe Suite restaurant & bar**, shop, viewing terraces, and free-roaming **Barbary macaques** (Europe's only wild monkeys, ~200+). The **Ape's Den** is at the **Middle Station** — note that between roughly **April and October the cable car does not stop at the Middle Station**. Nearby Upper Rock attractions: the **Skywalk** (glass platform ~340 m up), **St Michael's Cave**, Great Siege Tunnels. [naturereserve.gi], [gibraltar.com]

## Gibraltar International Airport & the Runway Crossing

- **The famous fact (now historical for road traffic).** Since the 1940s, **Winston Churchill Avenue** — the main road to the Spain border — crossed the **active runway** at grade, and **road (and rail-style) barriers stopped traffic** for every landing/take-off (more than ~15 times a day), causing tailbacks.
- **What changed — the Kingsway tunnel (2023).** On **31 March 2023** the **Kingsway** dual carriageway opened, taking road traffic through a **~350 m tunnel under the eastern end of the runway**. **Vehicular traffic no longer routinely stops for aircraft** — the at-grade crossing closed to everyday road traffic. [gibraltar.gov.gi 177/2023 & 187/2023], [Wikipedia: Gibraltar International Airport]
- **What still crosses at grade.** **Pedestrians, cyclists and mobility scooters** can still cross the runway surface at the old level crossing **when it is open** (a shorter route than the tunnel), and it stays available for exceptional/emergency vehicle use. When an aircraft operates, the level crossing is **still closed and signalled by barriers** for those pedestrians/cyclists. A subway/underpass alternative is provided. [Wikipedia], [gov.gi]
- **Safety record.** No serious vehicle–aircraft collision in 80+ years of operation. [AeroXplorer]
- **Net for Rockway:** a "runway-crossing live status" feature is now most relevant for **pedestrians/cyclists** at the old crossing, not general car drivers (who use the tunnel). Frame it accordingly.

## Ferries

- **No regular Gibraltar↔Spain passenger ferry to count on.** There is **no reliable scheduled passenger ferry between Gibraltar and Algeciras**; most cross-strait traffic runs from **Spanish ports (Algeciras, Tarifa)**, not Gibraltar. (A small Algeciras catamaran link has been referenced historically but is not a dependable mainstream service today.) Travellers from Gibraltar to Algeciras normally go **by road/bus across the frontier**. [Ferry search results, Wikipedia]
- **Gibraltar ↔ Tangier Med (Morocco).** **FRS** operates this route, but **service is sporadic/limited** — roughly **once or twice a week (often Fridays)**, sometimes only ~1–2 sailings per month, ~1.5 h crossing, fares from ~€37.50. It was suspended during COVID, resumed in 2022, and has continued on a low-frequency basis into 2026. **Treat schedule as volatile and always confirm live.** [Ferryhopper], [GBC], [gibraltar.gov.gi 288/2022]
- **Cruise.** Gibraltar is primarily a **cruise port of call** rather than a scheduled ferry hub.

## Parking

- **Scarcity.** Parking is genuinely scarce in Gibraltar; managing it is a stated government priority via the Transport and Parking Plan. [gibraltar.gov.gi]
- **Residential Parking Scheme (RPS) zones.** Rolled out progressively — **Zones 1, 2, 3 and 4**. Permit pricing escalates per household: **1st permit £5/month, 2nd £10/month, 3rd £20/month**, doubling for each further permit (to discourage multi-car households). [gibraltar.gov.gi], [GBC/Chronicle]
- **Pay & Display.** Pay-and-display bays operate within/around the zones for visitors and commercial users.
- **App.** **Gibraltar Car Parks Ltd** (gibcarparks.com) provides an **app and online portal** to buy/top-up/stop pay-and-display tickets, renew, and get expiry alerts; commercial and zone permits are also handled online. [gibcarparks.com]

## Design implications for Rockway

1. **Replace ride-hailing with GTA taxi booking.** Do **not** ship an Uber/Bolt-style "request a nearby driver" flow. Model taxis around the **Gibraltar Taxi Association**: a "call/book a taxi" action (phone/booking link), a **taxi-rank locator** (Casemates Square, Cathedral Square, airport, frontier, cruise terminal), and a dedicated **"Rock Tour" booking** product (shared per-person vs. private per-vehicle vs. VIP hourly).
2. **Surface government-regulated taxi fares, not surge pricing.** Show the official metered tariff/zone bands (pulled from the Transport Commission) and a clear "agree fare upfront for airport/tours" note. No dynamic pricing.
3. **Cable car must show a CLOSED state.** Hard-code/feature-flag the cable car as **closed for refurbishment until ~2027**, and route users to **taxi tour or walking** to reach the Upper Rock. Build the cable car attraction page so it can flip to "open" with hours/prices on reopening. Don't present live ticketing for a service that isn't running.
4. **Reframe the runway-crossing live status.** The headline feature should target **pedestrians/cyclists** crossing the old level crossing (still barrier-controlled for aircraft), not car drivers — cars now use the **Kingsway tunnel** and don't stop. A "runway crossing open/closed" indicator + "aircraft due" alert fits the walking/cycling layer; clearly tell drivers the tunnel is unaffected.
5. **Bus layer: small, fixed route set + free-travel logic.** Hard-model the ~8 routes (esp. **Route 5** frontier/airport shuttle as the key tourist route). Integrate **track.bus.gi** for live positions. Encode fare tiers and the **free travel for residents/commuters/military** eligibility, plus the **Hopper** day pass for tourists. Default trip-planner to bus across the frontier rather than ferry.
6. **Ferries = low-confidence, confirm-live.** Don't promise scheduled Gibraltar ferries. Present Tangier Med (FRS) as **occasional (1–2×/week, often Friday)** with a "verify availability" CTA, and steer Spain-bound users to road/bus + Spanish ports. Avoid a permanent "ferries" tab that implies frequent service.
7. **Parking: permit + pay-and-display, integrate the existing app.** Reflect **RPS Zones 1–4** with the escalating household permit pricing, surface **pay-and-display** bays, and integrate/deep-link **Gibraltar Car Parks (gibcarparks.com)** for purchase/top-up/expiry alerts rather than building a parallel payment system. Emphasize scarcity (real-time availability if obtainable).
8. **General principle.** Gibraltar transport is **small, government-regulated, and operator-concentrated** (one taxi association, one main bus company + Calypso, one car-park operator, no ride-hailing, minimal ferries). Rockway should **integrate with these incumbents** rather than replicate global-app patterns.
