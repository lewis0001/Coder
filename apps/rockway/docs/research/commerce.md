# Commerce in Gibraltar — Research

Research compiled 2026-05-30 for the Rockway "everything app". Facts are sourced and dated;
markets and businesses change, so treat specific names as accurate as of mid-2026 and verify
before any hard-coded use. Gibraltar is a small British Overseas Territory (~34,000 people,
~6.7 km²), so its commerce scene is compact: a handful of named venues dominate each category.

## Food & Restaurants

**Dining areas** (each is a real, walkable district):
- **Main Street** — pedestrian high street; mix of British, Mediterranean, cafes, confectioners.
- **Grand Casemates Square** — the big open-air dining/nightlife hub at the north end of Main Street.
- **Irish Town** — side street off Main Street with bars and restaurants.
- **Ocean Village** — modern marina with international cuisine and sea views (also a casino).
- **Marina Bay / Queensway Quay** — waterfront marinas, al-fresco dining (e.g. Casa Pepe, The Landings at Queensway Quay).
- **Catalan Bay (La Caleta)** — east-side fishing-village beach, seafood spots.

**Notable named venues** (illustrative, verify): Roy's Cod Place (fish & chips, Casemates),
Gauchos Steakhouse, AquaTerra (Casemates), Casa Pepe (Queensway Quay tapas),
The Landings (Queensway Quay), Gibraltar Confectionery (228 Main Street, sells calentita/cakes).

**Local food** (Gibraltarian cuisine blends British, Genoese/Italian, Spanish, Maltese, Moroccan):
- **Calentita** — the *national dish*: oven-baked chickpea-flour, water, olive oil, salt & pepper "pancake/farinata" (Genoese origin, 16th c.). Lends its name to the food festival.
- **Panissa** — fried set chickpea paste (Italian-descent).
- **Rosto** — penne in tomato sauce with beef/pork, mushrooms, carrots, cheese ("arrosto").
- **Fish & chips** — strong British staple presence.
- **Torta de Acelga** — Swiss-chard pie (Easter). **Rolitos** — beef olives. **Japonesa** — custard doughnut. **Bollo de Hornasso** — sweet aniseed bread (Easter/Christmas).

**Food delivery / ordering services operating in Gibraltar** (all local, not Deliveroo/Just Eat):
- **NomNoms** (nomnoms.gi) — local food ordering & delivery app, loyalty scheme.
- **Hungry Monkey** (hungrymonkey.gi) — broadest, 75+ eateries, loyalty cards, cuisine filters.
- **Rock Hero** (rockhero.gi) — local delivery service.
- **La Parrilla On The Go** — restaurant-own online ordering/delivery.
- Uber Eats has limited/uncertain presence; the local apps dominate.

## Supermarkets, Grocery & Pharmacy

- **Morrisons** — Queensway Road; the only hypermarket on the Rock and the dominant "big shop". Has in-store cafe, **pharmacy** and **petrol station**. UK products/brands. (No home delivery as of recent reports.)
- **Eroski City** — 12 Winston Churchill Avenue; Spanish franchise but stocks many Waitrose products; **offers home delivery**.
- **Coviran** — Andalusian chain; two branches (Irish Town and Waterport Road); local/Spanish/UK-imported goods, ~8:00–20:00 Mon–Sat.
- Plus smaller local shops/minimarkets. Prices typically run ~10–20% above Spain due to import costs.
- Pharmacies: Morrisons pharmacy plus independent chemists on/around Main Street.

## Classifieds / Marketplace

Gibraltarians lean heavily on **Facebook**:
- **Facebook Marketplace** (Gibraltar) and groups such as **"Gibraltar Buy & Sell"** and **"SECOND HAND GOODS FOR SALE GIBRALTAR"**.
- **GiBoard** (giboard.gi) — local buy/sell/classifieds site spanning real estate, vehicles, jobs, electronics.
- General community directories: GibYellow (gibyellow.gi), Angloinfo Gibraltar directory.

## Property

Compact, expensive market (limited land; demand from finance/gaming sectors). Main agents/portals:
- **Chestertons Gibraltar** (chestertons.gi) — largest by staff, award-winning; sales & lettings.
- **BMI Group** (bmigroup.gi) — leading agent; sales, rentals, commercial.
- **Seekers Property** (seekerspropertygibraltar.com).
- **BFA Estate Agents** (bfagib.com).
- **Property Gibraltar** (propertygibraltar.com) — aggregating **portal** carrying listings from many agents.
- Notable developments: Ocean Village, Marina Bay, Queensway Quay, Tribune/E1, Midtown. Prices are high relative to nearby Spain; rentals dominated by professional-sector tenants.

## Jobs

- **Government of Gibraltar** (gibraltar.gov.gi/vacancies and /press/job-opportunities) — civil service & Gibraltar Health Authority recruitment.
- **RecruitGibraltar** (recruitgibraltar.com) — longest-established agency (since 2004); strong in online gaming, financial services, insurance, IT, customer service.
- Job boards: Indeed (uk.indeed.com), Jooble, TipTopJob (gi.tiptopjob.com).
- Print/press: vacancies also advertised in the **Gibraltar Chronicle**.
- Key sectors: online gaming/betting, financial services, insurance, shipping/bunkering, tourism, public sector.

## Events & Culture

- **Gibraltar National Day — 10 September** (most significant): commemorates the 1967 sovereignty referendum; people wear **red & white** (the flag/national colours); political identity event. Activities: children's fancy-dress at Parliament (Main Street), street party at **John Mackintosh Square**, live music at Governor's Parade (Piazzela) and Casemates, children's funday at Casemates, and a fireworks display from the Detached Mole in the harbour.
- **Calentita Food Festival** — big open-air food & culture night, **Casemates Square** (typically June); celebrates local + Moroccan/Indian/British food with live music.
- **Gibraltar Music Festival** — paid music event (rock/pop/indie), early September around National Day, at Victoria Stadium.
- **Gibraltar International Literary Festival** — annual (Nov); venues like the Garrison Library and The Convent.
- **Spring Festival / regattas / sailing** — at the marinas.
- **Main venues:** Grand Casemates Square, **Victoria Stadium**, **Tercentenary Hall** (sports hall), John Mackintosh Hall (cultural centre), Garrison Library, The Convent.

## Local News Outlets

- **GBC — Gibraltar Broadcasting Corporation** (gbc.gi) — public TV, radio and online news.
- **Gibraltar Chronicle** (chronicle.gi) — daily paper (founded 1801; ex-Sundays); the paper of record.
- **Panorama** (gibraltarpanorama.gi) — independent weekly, free.
- Others: **Your Gibraltar TV / YGTV** (yourgibraltartv.com), GibNews, Vox.

## Design implications for Rockway

Use authentic local names/categories so the app feels native to the Rock:

- **Eat** — Browse by real dining districts as filters: *Main Street, Casemates Square, Irish Town, Ocean Village, Marina Bay, Queensway Quay, Catalan Bay*. Feature a "Gibraltarian / Local" cuisine tag surfacing **calentita, rosto, panissa, fish & chips, rolitos**. Model delivery on the local players (**Hungry Monkey, NomNoms, Rock Hero**) with loyalty-stamp mechanics; don't assume Deliveroo/Just Eat. Seed with venues like Roy's Cod Place, Gauchos, Casa Pepe, The Landings.
- **Shop** — Lead with the three real grocery anchors: **Morrisons** (hypermarket, cafe + pharmacy + petrol), **Eroski City** (Waitrose stock, has delivery), **Coviran** (2 branches). Note pharmacy lives inside Morrisons; surface Main Street retail.
- **Marketplace** — Mirror the Facebook-group habit: a "Buy & Sell" board with second-hand goods, plus categories matching **GiBoard** (vehicles, electronics, household, jobs). Free listings expected.
- **Property** — Aggregate like **Property Gibraltar**, badged by agent (**Chestertons, BMI, Seekers, BFA**). Use real developments as location filters (Ocean Village, Marina Bay, Queensway Quay, Midtown). Reflect high price level vs Spain.
- **Jobs** — Categories by Gibraltar's real economy: **online gaming, financial services, insurance, shipping/bunkering, public sector, tourism, IT**. Split "Government vacancies" (gov.gi) from agency/private. Reference RecruitGibraltar style.
- **Events** — Make **National Day (10 Sep, red & white)** a flagship. Pre-load the cultural calendar: **Calentita (June), Music Festival (Sep), Literary Festival (Nov)**, regattas. Venue picker: Casemates, John Mackintosh Square, Victoria Stadium, Tercentenary Hall.
- **News** — Aggregate the three real outlets: **GBC, Gibraltar Chronicle, Panorama** (+ YGTV). Lead with GBC (broadcast) and Chronicle (paper of record).
